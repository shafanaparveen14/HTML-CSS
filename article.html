Pollution

Introduction


Pollution is the introduction of harmful contaminants—such as chemicals, waste,

and smoke—into the natural environment, severely threatening air, water, and soil quality

Major types of pollution

Major Types of Pollution Air Pollution: Caused by emissions from factories, vehicles, and burning fossil fuels, releasing harmful gases like methane and carbon dioxide. Water Pollution: Contamination of water bodies with industrial waste, plastic, and chemicals, affecting aquatic life and human drinking water. Soil/Land Pollution: The dumping of waste, pesticides, and chemicals into the earth, which damages ecosystems and food safety.
